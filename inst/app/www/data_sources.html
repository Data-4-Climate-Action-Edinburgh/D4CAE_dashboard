Acknowledgments

Rainfall

The rainfall data is made available under an Open Government Licence by the Scottish Environment Protection Agency, SEPA. The files were downloaded from: https://www2.sepa.org.uk/rainfall/

Active Travel

Both walking and cycling data were from the Cycling Open Data (COD) portal on usmart. This portal is run by Cycling Scotland, and hosts over a hundred datasets. The Edinburgh cycling and walking data used were all provided to COD by the City of Edinburgh Council. The files were downloaded from: https://usmart.io/org/cyclingscotland/
